# My project brief

## The question
Which wards in Bosso LGA, Niger State are more than 5 km from a health facility?

## Why it matters
Health facility access shapes outcomes for the people of Bosso LGA. A clear picture of which wards fall outside a reasonable distance from care can inform local planning conversations and serves as the foundation project for my GeoDev Lab year.

## The data I need
- Ward boundaries — GRID3 NGA Operational Wards v3.0 — https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-operational-wards-v3-0/about — GeoPackage
- Health facility locations — GRID3 NGA Health Facilities v3.0 — https://data.grid3.org/datasets/827e3638dc204f4b9ddbbd19b00954d6/about — GeoPackage
- Roads (optional, for later travel-distance upgrade) — GRID3 NGA Roads v1.0 — https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-roads-v1-0/about

## What I would build
A map showing wards in Bosso LGA shaded by whether they fall more than 5 km from the nearest health facility. Eventually, a version using road travel distance instead of straight-line distance.
